# Hero assets

Photos for the landing page hero: the paddle and ball that break out of the glass card (see `docs/DESIGN.md` §5).

## What to put here
- **Paddle:** three-quarter angle (not flat-on), handle visible, sharp, at least 1500 px on the long side.
- **Ball:** on its own, sharp, holes clearly visible.
- Soft, even lighting with no harsh shadows.
- A transparent background (PNG) if possible:
  - iPhone: open the photo, long-press the paddle, then tap Copy or Share to get a cut-out.
  - Android: the "extract object" tool in Samsung Gallery or Google Photos.
  - Or use remove.bg.
  - A plain white or single-color background also works. Claude will cut it out.
- Only your own photos or the court's paddles. No images from paddle brands' websites (copyrighted, and they carry brand logos).

Name them simply, e.g. `paddle.png`, `ball.png`. Extra angles are welcome.

Claude makes the cleaned-up, compressed versions the app uses. These originals stay here as the source.
